Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: —

We want you to be happy with your letter. Here's our simple, fair policy.

Digital product — instant delivery

A personalised letter from Santa is a digital product that is created and delivered immediately after your payment. Because of this, orders generally cannot be cancelled once the letter has been generated and delivered.

When we give a refund

We will gladly resend your letter, or give you a full refund, if:

  • Your payment was taken but the letter was not delivered due to a technical problem on our side.
  • You were charged more than once for the same order (we refund the duplicate).
  • There was a clear error we caused that we cannot fix by resending.

How to request a refund

Email youremail@gmail.com within 7 days of your purchase. Please include:

  • The email you used at checkout
  • The Razorpay Payment ID (shown after payment / in your payment receipt)
  • A short note on what went wrong

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to your original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5–7 business days. The exact timing depends on your bank.

Tip: if your letter simply didn't arrive by email, check your spam folder first — then contact us and we'll resend it free.
