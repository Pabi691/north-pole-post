Privacy Policy

Last updated: —

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

Information we collect

  • Letter details you type: the child's first name, age (optional), town/city (optional) and the good deed.
  • Your email address, so we can send you the letter.
  • Payment information is handled entirely by Razorpay. We receive only a confirmation that payment succeeded — we never see or store your card number, CVV or UPI PIN.

How we use it

  • To create and deliver your personalised letter.
  • To email the letter to the address you provide.
  • To respond to support requests.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted services needed to run the shop:

  • Razorpay — to process your payment securely.
  • Our email provider — to deliver your letter.

How long we keep it

We keep order details only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal requirements, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Security

Payments run over secure, encrypted connections through Razorpay. We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us.

Children's information

Letters are purchased by adults (parents or guardians) on a child's behalf. We only use a child's first name and details to personalise the letter, and for no other purpose.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information. Just email youremail@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us via our Contact page.
